# Amharic Bible Commentary Sources Research

This document tracks and records the verification and licensing research for various Bible commentary leads for the Birhan Bible app. As per project guidelines, no commentary data will be bundled or processed without explicit rights clearance and quality assurance.

---

## Commentary — Licensing Targets (Phase 2+)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **GATE:** Do not pursue or send permission requests for these targets until the app has **three pilot churches live**. This ensures we approach rights-holders as an established Bible app actively serving Ethiopian congregations rather than a prototype.

### 1. Primary Target: Thomas Hale, *Applied New Testament Commentary (Amharic)*
* **Description:** Single-author, single-volume, evangelical, plain-language commentary covering the New Testament only. It is the absolute best conceptual fit for a mobile Bible study app.
* **Licensing Status:** In copyright. The Amharic edition was published by "Bible Society (2004)," which is almost certainly the **Bible Society of Ethiopia (BSE)**. 
* **Acquisition Strategy:** Since we already need to negotiate with the BSE for the Amharic Bible text rights (spec §7), we will fold this commentary request into the same BSE conversation as a second ask. This keeps the project's institutional relationships consolidated (one relationship, two assets).
* **Current Status:** No direct permissions contact verified yet; no downloadable text. Requires a formal licensing request.

### 2. Secondary Target: *Africa Bible Commentary (Amharic)*
* **Description:** Richer and broader in scope, but features over 100 contributors across 30+ countries, making rights clearance and royalty distributions materially harder.
* **Licensing Status:** In copyright. Distributed via Langham Publishing / Zondervan Academic. A paid license is likely required.
* **Acquisition Strategy:** Pursue only as a backup if negotiations for the Thomas Hale commentary fall through.
* **Current Status:** No direct permissions contact verified yet; no downloadable text. Requires a formal licensing request.

## Detailed Source Verification (Current Leads)

### 1. William MacDonald: Believer's Bible Commentary (BBC)
* **Lead Link:** [william-macdonald.org/bbc-translations](https://www.william-macdonald.org/bbc-translations/)
* **Translator / Publisher:** Original English by William MacDonald. The official translation list does not identify any translator or publisher for Amharic. 
* **Presence of Amharic Translation:** **Absent**. Amharic is completely missing from the list of published translations, and is not listed in the "Work is in progress" section. Its existence under official MacDonald copyrights is unconfirmed.
* **License Status & Stated Permissions Process:** The page does not explicitly define a general permissions process or contact channel for rights inquiries, nor does it host a contact form. It only lists contact emails for individual publishers of existing language translations, plus a plain-text note: *"Please inform the webmaster if you cannot contact the publishers. Thanks."*
* **Structured Format Availability:** No structured or downloadable formats are mentioned for Amharic since the language is absent from the page. Downloads for existing languages are noted as typically available in **PDF format** via third-party websites like `sermon-online.org` or `plymouthbrethren.org`.
* **Verdict:** **Pursue (Inquire Only)** — Inquire whether an Amharic translation exists, is planned, or if they are aware of a licensed print edition in Ethiopia. Do not request text as though it exists in their catalog.
* **Next Action:** Draft a permission and existence inquiry email to the rights holders using their official contact channels.

### 2. HistoricalChristianFaith/Commentaries-Database
* **Lead Link:** [github.com/HistoricalChristianFaith/Commentaries-Database](https://github.com/HistoricalChristianFaith/Commentaries-Database)
* **Translator / Publisher:** Open-source community compilation.
* **License Status:** Public Domain / Open Source.
* **Language & Content Fit:** English. The database focuses on Early Church Fathers (patristic commentaries, Catena Aurea, Thomas Aquinas, Augustine, etc.).
* **Theological / Language Fit:** Poor fit. The text is entirely in English with no Amharic translation. Furthermore, the patristic focus (predominantly Catholic/Eastern Orthodox historical theology) is not suited for the target Protestant/Evangelical Amharic audience of the Birhan Bible app.
* **Verdict:** **Reject**
* **Next Action:** None. Do not revisit.

### 3. Ethiopiansite.com Commentary App
* **Lead Link:** ethiopiansite.com (YeHiwot Injera / Bread of Life ministry)
* **Translator / Publisher:** YeHiwot Injera (Bread of Life) ministry.
* **License Status:** Copyrighted by the ministry. The text is locked inside a compiled Android application (APK).
* **Extraction Feasibility:** Out of scope and rejected. Extracting the text from the APK would constitute a copyright violation and is technically outside the scope of this project.
* **Verdict:** **Reject**
* **Next Action:** None. Do not revisit.

### 4. HUC Resources / Biblenerd Awesome Bible Developer Resources
* **Lead Link:** [github.com/biblenerd/awesome-bible-developer-resources](https://github.com/biblenerd/awesome-bible-developer-resources)
* **Translator / Publisher:** Curated by the developer community (biblenerd / Hebrew Union College).
* **License Status:** Open source resource listings.
* **Scope Fit:** This is a developer-focused curation of metadata formats, translation tools (e.g., USFM, OSIS, Zefania), original language databases, and APIs. It does not contain Amharic commentary content.
* **Verdict:** **Out of scope**
* **Next Action:** None. Do not revisit.

## Closed / Rejected Sources (Do Not Re-litigate)
* **Anonymous BibleCommentaries EPUB:** Rejected due to lack of clear ownership/licensing rights and major text completeness issues (large portions of text left untranslated or mixed in English).
* **worlddic.com `#googtrans` Category:** Rejected. These are scraped resources relying on automated web-browser translations (Google Translate widget) rather than dedicated biblical translation.
* **EOTC / Traditional Commentary (e.g., Andemta):** Rejected as a theological fit. These resources represent traditional Ge'ez/Orthodox scholarship, which does not align with the Protestant/Evangelical target audience of the app.
* **Machine Translation as a Method:** Formally rejected. Raw machine translations are unacceptable for our bundled commentaries due to accuracy issues and theological risk.
